Register

Only software and networks register. Pick your participant type below. Observers and anyone else just join the Zoom.

Registrations are public. Each registration is a GitHub issue that anyone can read, and it needs a free GitHub account. Use synthetic data only. Don't enter real health information, credentials, or secrets. For contact, use a Slack handle or a shared inbox if you don't want a personal email shown.

Each button opens a short form for that type. Submit it once per component you bring. If you bring more than one, for example a network and an app, register each separately.

What happens next

  1. The organizers review your registration and add it to the Participant directory.
  2. Update your registration by editing the issue, for example when your status changes from planned to up.
  3. Find counterparts in the directory and pair up for the scenarios.

What to prepare

Participant typeHave ready
NetworkYour $match (RLS) endpoint, your Dynamic Client Registration /register endpoint, the software statement types you accept, a FHIR base URL for data queries, and the scenarios you plan to run.
ProviderYour FHIR base URL or how testers reach it, your home network if you connect through one, and whether you respond to queries, send them, or both.
EHR vendorThe product you are bringing, its FHIR base URL, your home network if any, and whether you also publish to or validate against the National Provider Directory.
PayerYour FHIR base URL, your home network if any, and which of claims and benefit queries, clinical data queries, and quality queries you take part in.
Patient-facing appYour Medicare App Library status, how counterparts can test the app, and your home network and IAL2 identity provider if you have them.

Every registration also asks for your organization, component name, status (up, in progress, or planned), a contact for pairing, and any notes for partners.
