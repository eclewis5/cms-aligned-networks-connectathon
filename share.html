Share your experience

Write a short report on what you tried, what worked, what was hard, and what you'd change.

Open the feedback formA short Google Form. It takes about five minutes.Open the form →

Reports are public, credited with the name and organization you give. Use synthetic data only. Don't enter real health information, tokens, or secrets.

How to share your experience

  1. Open the feedback form. You don't need to sign in.
  2. Enter your name, your organization, and the role you played.
  3. Answer the questions below. Only the first is required, so skip any that don't apply.
  4. Submit the form. Send one report per organization, or one per role if you played more than one.

To record the result of a single scenario run, use the Results sheet instead.

Questions to answer

  1. What did you test, and with whom?
  2. What worked the first time?
  3. What failed, and what was the cause?
  4. Which GA requirement was unclear or hard to implement?
  5. What would you change in the requirements, the tools, or the event?

Report-out

The event closes with a report-out in the main Zoom room where participants share what they found. Bring one finding.
