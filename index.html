CMS Aligned Networks Connectathon

The CMS Aligned Networks Connectathon is an online event in January 2027 (exact date and time TBD) where networks, providers, EHR vendors, payers, and app developers test the network-level requirements of the CMS Health Tech Ecosystem (HTE) General Availability (GA) Requirements against each other, and tell us what they found.

Draft status. Scenarios are based on the CMS-Aligned Network Specification (can-spec/0.6, an editor's draft) and the GA Requirements. They will be rechecked against the current GA document before the event.

How the event works

  • What it's for. Real interoperability experience between CMS aligned networks, not a formal certification or conformance test. Participants run shared scenarios in pairs, and failures are as useful as passes because they often point to a gap in the requirements or an interoperability bug.
  • Zoom. Meeting link TBD. Everyone meets in the main room for the opening, the hourly check-ins, and the closing report-out.
  • Slack. Questions, pairing, and links go in channel TBD on the CMS Health Tech Ecosystem Slack.
  • Breakout rooms. Two people debugging together can start a Slack huddle in a direct message. A larger group can open a video room and post the link in the channel.
  • Only software and networks register. Teams bringing a network endpoint, client, or participant system register it (How to test). Everyone else just joins.
  • Made-up data only. Every patient, record, and organization used in the scenarios is synthetic. Never use real health information, even your own.

How to test

Participants bring one or more roles: a network (routes and answers queries across CMS aligned networks), a participant system (a provider, EHR, or payer endpoint that responds to or sends queries), or a patient-facing app (registers and queries on a patient's behalf). Make your component testable without you in the room, and list it in the Participant directory by registering.

Test early and alone against the reference test tools (tools TBD), so the live event is left for problems that need two networks. Run each scenario with as many counterparts as you can reach, and record every run.

Ground rules

  • One onboarding, accepted everywhere. Participants onboarded by one CMS aligned network must be accepted by the others without redundant onboarding. Networks may coordinate operations but must not add duplicate trust requirements.
  • Dynamic Client Registration is the default. The GA allows manual registration only through October 1, 2026. For this event, register clients dynamically.
  • Identity at IAL2 only. Patient-initiated queries use a CMS-approved IAL2 credential. No portal logins, and no extra identity steps beyond IAL2 token validation.
  • Purpose of Use travels with every request. Every query declares one of the HL7 Purpose of Use codes: PATRQT, TREAT, HPAYMT, CLMATTCH, HOPERAT, or HQUALIMP.
  • Patient matching uses the CMS-approved logic. If a query matches under any field combination in the CMS patient matching proposal, the receiver must respond, subject to applicable law.
  • Structured and unstructured data. Responses include USCDI v3 data and unstructured artifacts such as PDFs, scans, and images.
  • Law still applies. HIPAA and other applicable law govern every response. Withholding is allowed only where law permits it.

Sharing what you found

Everyone is invited to write a short experience report: what you tried, what worked, what was hard, and what you'd change. The Share your experience page explains how. Reports are public, credited with the name and organization you give, or anonymous.
