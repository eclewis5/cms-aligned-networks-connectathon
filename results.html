What the form asks for

  • Your name and organization.
  • The role you played: network, provider, EHR vendor, payer, patient-facing app, or observer.
  • The scenarios you tested, such as N3, and who you tested with.
  • The outcome (passed, partly passed, failed, or could not run), and what happened.
  • Your recommendations for the requirements, the tools, or the event.
  • Optionally, a screenshot, and where you can, the request and response (synthetic data only).
