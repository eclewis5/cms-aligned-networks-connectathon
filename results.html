Results

Every recorded scenario run, public, in one shared Google Sheet. A failure is as useful as a pass.

Open the results sheetRead everyone's results and add your own row.Open in Google Sheets →

The sheet is public and anyone with the link can edit it. What you enter is visible to everyone, including your name. Use synthetic data only. Don't enter real health information, tokens, or secrets. Add your own rows, and don't change or delete anyone else's.

How to share a result

  1. Open the results sheet.
  2. Go to the first empty row at the bottom of the table.
  3. Fill in every column, using the guide below. Add one row per scenario run, or per scenario if you ran it with several partners.
  4. For several scenarios, give each its own row. Don't combine them in one cell.
  5. Check that your row has no real health information or secrets.

What each column asks for

ColumnWhat to enter
DateThe day you ran the scenario, for example 2027-01-14.
NameYour name, shown publicly with your result.
OrganizationYour organization's name.
Role playedNetwork, Provider, EHR vendor, Payer, Patient-facing app, or Observer or other.
Scenarios testedOne scenario number, such as N3 or A1.
Who you tested withThe networks or components on the other side, and which side started the request.
Overall outcomePassed, Partly passed, Failed, or Could not run.
What happenedWhat you tried, what worked, what failed, and the cause if you know it. Say which pass criteria you met.
RecommendationsWhat you would change in the requirements, the test tools, or the event. Mention any unclear or hard-to-implement requirement.
EvidenceOptional. A link to a screenshot, or to a request and response. Synthetic data only, with tokens and secrets removed.
