Providers, EHR vendors, payers, and apps

You connect to a CMS aligned network, or build something that does. Join through your network, or bring your own endpoint and test it with the networks at the event.

The network-level scenarios are the focus of this event. Scenarios for provider, EHR, payer, and app responsibilities are TBD. What each role would be expected to do, from the GA Requirements, is below so you can prepare.

Providers

  • Respond to treatment queries (provider to provider) with all clinical and claims data held, structured and unstructured, unless legally restricted.
  • Respond to patient access queries authenticated with a CMS-approved IAL2 credential, with no portal login and no extra HIPAA authorization.
  • Respond to payer queries for clinical data supporting claims from the last 60 days, and to quality queries (HQUALIMP) with the agreed measure data.
  • Honor agreed disclosure restrictions under 45 CFR 164.522.
  • Keep National Provider Directory information (endpoints and verification data) complete and current, directly or through your EHR vendor or network.
  • Provide audit event data to the network.

EHR vendors

  • Accept IAL2 identity tokens and never require portal credentials for patient access requests.
  • Apply the CMS-approved patient matching logic.
  • Support FHIR query and response for patient access (PATRQT), treatment (TREAT), and payment (HPAYMT), delivering structured and unstructured data.
  • Integrate with the National Provider Directory to publish and to validate inbound requests.
  • Return payer clinical and quality query responses by query, not by proactive push.
  • Capture and provide audit events (who, when, Purpose of Use, network-side participant identity).

Payers

  • Respond to patient, provider, and payer queries for claims and benefit data, with no portal accounts, and by query rather than by proactive push.
  • Query providers for clinical data supporting claims from the last 60 days.
  • Query for measure-specific data under HQUALIMP.
  • Accept IAL2 identity for patient queries without portal login.
  • Publish endpoints, identity, and contact metadata to the National Provider Directory, and apply the CMS-approved patient matching logic.
  • Log requests and responses (who, when, Purpose of Use) and retain them for at least 7 years.

Patient-facing apps

  • Verify identity through a CMS-approved IAL2 credential service provider.
  • Register with CMS aligned networks using Dynamic Client Registration and a CMS-signed software statement (Medicare App Library apps).
  • Source clinical and claims data from at least one CMS aligned network, with no portal sign-in.
  • Store identity tokens, let patients set query specificity once and change it any time, and show audit logs for 7 years.
  • Meet the CARIN Code of Conduct and hold DirectTrust or DiME certification, plus any later CMS review.
  • Apps in the Kill the Clipboard, Conversational AI, and Diabetes/Obesity categories have additional requirements. See the GA Requirements.
